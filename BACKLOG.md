## Lesson 15 — Равновесие на телата

- Gravity arrow length: G is a fixed 0,45 m, so on a low box (or a box lying
  on the ground) it pokes into the ramp / ground. Scale it to the box or
  clip it at the surface.
- Tumbling extension: landings are fully inelastic (the box stops dead on
  each face, no bounce or carried-over spin), and after rolling over twice
  the status still says "на страната си" although the box is on an end.
- Sliding before tipping: a real box slides when tan θ > μ. Currently ruled
  out by the "гумена подложка" assumption.
- Predict-then-reveal: three boxes on different faces on one board; guess
  the order they fall in, then tilt.
- Ball-on-hill widget: nudge a ball on a hilltop / in a valley / on flat
  ground (unstable / stable / neutral equilibrium).
